# Practice Player Privacy Policy

**Meatloaf For Everyone LLC** · Effective October 3, 2026 · Last updated October 3, 2026

This policy explains what Practice Player (the "App") does with your information. The App is made by Meatloaf For Everyone LLC ("we", "us"), a one-person company in Colorado, USA, that decides how this data is used. Contact: general@meatloafforeveryone.com.

## The short version

- Your songs, recordings, loops, notes, playlists, and practice stats stay on your device unless you turn on iCloud sync (off by default), export or share something, or send us feedback.
- Data comes to us in three ways: usage analytics tied to a code for your install, not your name, which the App cannot turn off; feedback and emails you choose to send; and crash reports Apple shares with developers if you allow it in iOS Settings. Song, playlist, and file names can appear in feedback and error reports. We never receive your Apple ID, or the audio of your recordings unless you attach a file to an email yourself.
- Deleting the App does not delete data in your iCloud, songs it added to your Apple Music library, backups you exported, anything already sent to us, or two small keychain items (section 1).
- You can ask to see, correct, or delete your data, or object to analytics, by emailing us (section 7).
- We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Section 6 lists everyone who receives it.

## 1. What the App stores on your device

Songs (references to your Apple Music library and copies of audio files you import), recordings, practice data (loop points, notes, playlists, per-song settings, practice stats, goals, reminder schedules), settings, purchase status, a diagnostic log, a crash log if the App crashes, and the email address you last typed into the feedback form.

Deleting the App removes this from the device, except two keychain items: a count of your purchased export credits, which the App also keeps in iCloud whether or not sync is on, so the credits survive a reinstall; and, if you used older versions, the App version you first installed.

When your device is connected to a computer, the App's files (audio, recordings, logs, and exported backups) can be copied in Finder or the Apple Devices app. A backup holds your songs' details but no audio and no playlists.

## 2. Permissions

- **Media & Apple Music**: to play songs in your library, add catalog songs you pick, and suggest songs in "For You". Apple handles these requests (section 5).
- **Microphone**: used only while you are recording.
- **Notifications**: used only for practice reminders, which are scheduled on the device. We do not send push notifications.

You can change these in iOS Settings (System Settings on a Mac).

## 3. Usage analytics

The App from the App Store or TestFlight sends usage signals to [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/), an analytics service in Germany with servers in the European Union, so we can see which features are used and how often errors happen. Each signal contains:

- An event name (for example "loop created") with small details such as a count, a setting, or a product, including purchases, refunds, and their prices.
- For errors, a technical message that can include the name of the song, playlist, or file involved, or a file's full location, which on a Mac includes your account name.
- Device context: model, iOS and App version, language, region, time zone, screen and display settings, and how often and how long the App is used.
- A scrambled code that stays the same for each install, made from an ID iOS gives the App, so repeated signals count as one user. TelemetryDeck scrambles it again, and it is not linked to your name, email, or Apple ID.

This data is pseudonymous: it identifies one install over time but is not linked to your name. Signals do not include your email, Apple ID, or recordings.

We rely on our legitimate interest in fixing bugs and improving the App. The App has no switch to turn analytics off, and because we cannot tell which install is yours, signals continue after you object. If you object, we will search for signals matching the details you give us and ask TelemetryDeck to delete them (section 7).

## 4. Feedback you send us

When you tap Submit on the feedback form, the App sends us your message, your email address if you give one, whether you turned on "OK to send me news & updates" (off by default), App and device details, the open song's title and identifiers or file location, a summary of your sync status and library size, and the App's diagnostic and crash logs. The song details and logs are always included; to leave them out, email us directly instead.

Feedback goes to a script hosted by Google and is saved in our Google account. If the form cannot reach Google, or when you use Send Feedback in the player menu, the review prompt, or after a crash, the App opens your email app instead, with your message, App and device details, and the sync summary filled in; Apple Mail also attaches the logs. Nothing is sent until you send it.

We use feedback to answer you and fix the App, and keep it only as long as that takes. If you turned on news & updates, we keep your email address to occasionally write to you until you reply "stop". Feedback is stored as you send it, so please leave out anything sensitive. To delete a submission, email us from the address you used, or tell us roughly when you sent it and what it said.

## 5. Apple services

Apple Music requests, App Store purchases, and theme downloads are handled by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Adding a catalog song can add it to your Apple Music library. We never receive your payment details or Apple ID; the App checks purchases on the device.

**iCloud sync (optional, off by default).** When on, your practice data is stored in your private iCloud database and your audio files and recordings move to the App's private iCloud storage, so your other devices can use them. We have no access to either. Turning sync off takes effect after you restart the App and leaves everything in iCloud, where your audio continues to play from. To remove it from all your devices, delete Practice Player's data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, and export anything you want to keep first.

## 7. Your rights and choices

- **See or delete your data**: most of it is on your device already. For iCloud, see section 5. For feedback, email us. For analytics, send us details such as when an error happened, your device model, or a playlist name, and we will search for matching signals and ask TelemetryDeck to delete them.
- **Object to analytics**: email us. The App has no switch for it.
- **Stop news emails**: reply "stop" or email us.
- **Stop Apple's crash sharing**: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.
- **Stop sync or reminders**: turn them off in the App.

If you are in the EU or UK, you can also see, correct, or delete your data, limit or object to how we use it, get a reusable copy, and withdraw consent at any time. You can complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO at ico.org.uk). Email us to use any right, wherever you live; we answer within one month and will not treat you differently for asking.

## 8. Other things you should know

- **Children**: The App is for general audiences and not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete their feedback and search for their analytics.
- **Where data is processed**: We are in the United States. Google, Cloudflare, and GitHub are US companies whose servers can be anywhere; TelemetryDeck processes analytics in the EU. Your data may be processed in a country with different privacy laws from yours.
- **Security**: The App's connections are encrypted (HTTPS). If a breach affects your data, we will tell the authorities where the law requires and tell you by email if we have your address, or on practiceplayer.app if not.
- **Our website**: [practiceplayer.app](https://practiceplayer.app) is hosted by Cloudflare, which sets a short-lived security cookie, and loads fonts from Google Fonts. It has no analytics or tracking.
- **Do Not Track**: The App and website do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so a Do Not Track signal changes nothing.
